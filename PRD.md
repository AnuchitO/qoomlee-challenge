# Project PRD: Qoomlee Airline App

## 1. Project Overview
**Qoomlee Airline** is a premium, modern aviation platform designed for the "Aero-Modernist" traveler. The app focuses on a seamless, high-fidelity experience from the initial flight search to boarding and account management. The visual language is defined by clarity, sophisticated use of whitespace, and a high-contrast palette that ensures legibility and a sense of "airiness" and modern efficiency.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Frequent Flyers:** Users who value speed, efficiency, and real-time updates.
- **Tech-Savvy Travelers:** Individuals who prefer digital-first solutions for booking, check-in, and document management.
- **Leisure Travelers:** Users seeking an intuitive, stress-free interface for planning and managing their trips.

## 3. Visual Identity & Design System
- **Design Theme:** Aero-Modernist.
- **Primary Color:** `#0e70ca` (Qoomlee Blue).
- **Typography:** Inter (Sans-serif).
- **Key Principles:**
    - **Elevation & Depth:** Use of soft shadows and cards to create a layered, organized interface.
    - **Information Hierarchy:** Bold headlines and clear status indicators (✅ Met / ⚠️ Action needed).
    - **Aero-Motifs:** Subtle gradients and iconography that evoke the feeling of flight.

## 4. Key User Flows & Screens

### A. Booking Flow (Challenge scope)
1. **Flight Search:** Entry point with clear departure/arrival inputs and trending destinations.
2. **Flight Results:** Multi-tier flight options (Qoomlee Air, SkyConnect, Oceanic Air) with price and duration clarity.
3. **Flight Details:** Deep dive into specific flight segments, baggage allowances, and cabin features.
4. **Passenger Info:** Detailed data entry for travelers with real-time field validation.
5. **Seat Selection:** Interactive seat map for choosing preferred locations (Window, Aisle, Extra Legroom).
6. **Travel Insurance:** Clear coverage options to provide peace of mind.
7. **Secure Payment:** SSL-encrypted checkout with summary of total costs.
8. **Booking Confirmation:** Success state with PNR code and immediate post-booking actions.

### B. Check-in Flow (Challenge scope)
1. **Online Check-in Portal:** Search by PNR or last name to retrieve active bookings.
2. **Select Passengers:** Choose which travelers are checking in for the upcoming flight.
3. **Check-in Details:** Multi-tab interface for Personal info, Baggage declaration, and Seat review.
4. **Review Check-in:** Final summary before issuing digital passes.
5. **Digital Boarding Pass:** High-fidelity pass with QR code, gate info, and Apple Wallet integration.

### C. Post-Booking & Management
1. **Booking Details Hub:** The central mission control for a confirmed trip.
2. **Pre-flight Checklist:** A task-oriented view helping users track visa status, document readiness, and airport timing.
3. **Flight Status:** Real-time flight tracking and airport board simulation.
4. **Refund Request:** Transparent cancellation flow with estimated refund calculations.

### D. Account & Support
1. **Login/Register:** Branded authentication flow.
2. **Account Settings:** Centralized profile management including personal info, travel documents, and payment methods.
3. **Customer Support Hub:** FAQ and multi-channel support (Chat, Email, Phone).
4. **Live Chat:** Real-time conversational support interface.

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Responsive Layout:** Mobile-first architecture with components that scale for tablet/desktop.
- **Accessibility:** High color contrast (WCAG AA compliant) and touch-friendly targets (min 44px).
- **Security:** PCI-DSS compliant payment interfaces and 256-bit SSL encryption for data handling.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Booking Conversion:** Percentage of users who complete the booking flow from search.
- **Check-in Adoption:** Ratio of digital vs. airport check-ins.
- **Self-Service Rate:** Volume of support requests resolved via the FAQ and Hub vs. Live Chat.
